# Hardware Components

## Main Components

- ESP32 development board
- L298N motor driver
- Four DC geared motors
- Four wheels
- Robot car chassis
- Battery
- Battery holder
- Power switch
- Jumper wires
- USB cable

## Component Functions

### ESP32

The ESP32 receives Bluetooth commands from a mobile phone and sends control signals to the L298N motor driver.

### L298N Motor Driver

The L298N controls the direction and movement of the DC motors.

### DC Geared Motors

Four geared motors move the car forward, backward, left, and right.

### Battery

The battery supplies power to the motor driver, motors, and ESP32.
